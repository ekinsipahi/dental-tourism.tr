## The short answer

Two visits. The first takes about a week: examination, tomography scan, the surgery and, if your bone allows it, a temporary tooth. Then the implant heals for about three months. The second visit takes about a week as well: impressions, try-in and the final teeth; if you choose a more aesthetic ceramic such as e.max, count on more than two weeks. That is the honest timeline for [dental implants](/treatments/dental-implants/) in our clinic in Şişli, Istanbul, and it does not change much whether you need one implant or a full jaw.

What you cannot do is finish the final teeth in a single trip. We explain why below, and what happens in each week, so that you can book your travel around real dates rather than around a promise.

**Key points**

- Two visits of about a week each, about three months apart.
- Week one ends with the implant in place and, if the bone allows it, a temporary tooth.
- If a tooth has to be extracted, the implant is placed in the same session; we do not make you wait months for that.
- The three-month gap is healing time: about two months in the lower jaw, three in the upper, and we wait three for both.
- Week two is impressions and the final teeth; aesthetic ceramics such as e.max take longer.
- Everything starts with your X-ray or scan, sent before you book anything.

The first visit begins with an examination in the chair. If you do not already have a tomography (CBCT) scan, one is taken, because the decision about an implant rests on what the scan shows about the density of your bone. The final plan is made at that examination and discussed with you before anything starts; what you received before you travelled was a preliminary assessment, and the examination is what turns it into a plan.

Then comes the surgery. Implants are placed by Dr. Devrim Biriken Sipahi or Dt. Ekin Sipahi, and for demanding cases by experienced oral surgeons who come to our clinic. If the bone is in good condition, a temporary tooth is fitted within the same week, so that you do not fly home with a gap. Whether the bone allows that is a judgement made on your scan and in your mouth, which is why we do not promise it in advance.

Before you leave, you are checked once more, and if painkillers are needed they are prescribed before you get out of the chair. Everything, including the temporary tooth, is finished within that first week.

## If a tooth has to be extracted first

Patients often expect that an extraction means a long wait before the implant. In our clinic it usually does not. If a tooth has to come out, the implant is placed in the same session. Waiting months after an extraction is no longer recommended by current research, because the bone around the empty socket resorbs; the longer the wait, the less bone is left, and in the end there may be no room to place an implant at all. So the extraction does not add a third trip. It happens inside week one.

## Why the final teeth cannot be made on the first trip

Immediate loading, which means a temporary tooth within the first week, is possible only if there is enough bone. The final teeth are a different matter. After placement the implant heals into the bone for about two months in the lower jaw and about three in the upper, and in practice we wait three months for both, because the fit of the final prosthesis depends on it. When a clinic forces the final teeth into a single trip, the fit of the prosthesis becomes the second question mark on top of the implant, and that is not a risk we take.

If you do not have enough bone, the timeline changes. We do sinus lifts with grafting; if the bone is still insufficient, short implants are an option, and for a back jaw where the bone has resorbed, All-on-4 can be considered. Which of these applies to you is something we tell you in the preliminary assessment, before you book, not on the day. We have written more about what makes an implant safe, including the health conditions and medicines that change the plan, in [our article on whether dental implants in Turkey are safe](/blog/safety-and-quality/is-it-safe-to-get-dental-implants-in-turkey/).

## The three months in between

You go home with the implant healing and, if the bone allowed it, a temporary tooth. Your job in that period is simple: look after the implants the way we show you. Ours is to stay in touch; we ask how you are doing, and not only once. If anything looks wrong, red gums around the implant, a sunken look under the tooth, an implant that moves, write to us at once with a photo. What happens then is described in [what we do if an implant fails after you return home](/blog/safety-and-quality/dental-implant-failed-after-returning-home/). Your own dentist at home can take over the follow-up if you prefer, and if you wish we explain the case to them directly.

A temporary tooth is a temporary tooth. It can break, and if it does, that is not a problem; it is replaced. It is there so that you can smile, not so that you can test it on hard food.

## Week two: impressions, try-in and the final teeth

About three months after the surgery you come back. Impressions are taken, the final teeth are made and fitted, and this normally takes about a week. If you choose a more aesthetic ceramic such as e.max, the work takes longer and goes beyond two weeks; those are the more aesthetic solutions, and they need the time. Whatever you choose, you see the result before it is fixed, and if you do not like the shape or the colour, it is changed. We make prostheses to last for many years, which is also why we do not rush this week.

## One implant, several, or a full jaw

The pattern is the same: two visits of about a week each, about three months apart. What changes is the number of appointments inside each week and, for a full jaw, the amount of planning that goes into the scan. If sinus lifts or grafts are needed, the timeline stretches, and you will know that before you book. If a clinic offers you a full jaw with final teeth in a single week, ask them how the implant is supposed to have healed.

## Before you book your flights

Send us your panoramic X-ray or tomography scan on WhatsApp or e-mail; the messages are answered by the clinic's dentists or their assistants. You receive a written preliminary assessment, free of charge: what is likely to be possible, how many visits and how long between them. Only then book your travel, around dates that are real. [How to send your X-ray and what the assessment covers](/blog/planning-your-trip/send-your-dental-x-ray-for-a-preliminary-assessment/) is explained step by step in a separate article.

## The timeline in one list

- **Before you travel:** X-ray or scan sent, health and medicines disclosed, written preliminary assessment, dates agreed.
- **Week one:** examination, tomography if needed, final plan, surgery (extraction in the same session if a tooth has to go), temporary tooth if the bone allows, check before you fly.
- **About three months:** healing at home; we stay in touch; you write to us if anything looks wrong.
- **Week two:** impressions, try-in, final teeth; more than two weeks for aesthetic ceramics such as e.max.
- **After that:** check-ups every six months, at least once a year, which your own dentist can do with a panoramic X-ray; we keep asking how you are doing, and if an implant fails, we place a new one and do not charge for it.
